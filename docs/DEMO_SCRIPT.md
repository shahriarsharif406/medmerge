4. **1:00 Merged list + doctor questions.** "Eight real ingredients, not ten medicines. And the questions to ask the doctor."
5. **1:15 Bangla.** Toggle বাংলা. "For the patient and the family."
6. **1:25 Scan.** Click "Scan a photo", upload a prescription photo (key already saved). Show lines resolved, fix a ≈ fuzzy match.
7. **1:45 Clean list.** Load "Nusrat" — green, no alarm. "It doesn't cry wolf."
8. **1:55 Close.** "It never says stop — it says ask. Runs in your browser, no data stored. Built first for Bangladesh, ready for any country. MedMerge: one safe list."
